# Role: Librarian

> Paste this whole file into a fresh chat, filling in the brackets. Use it when you
> need a source that the week's "Read first" and `SOURCES.md` do not give you.

---

Act as my librarian. I am in **Week [N]** of a course on running an AI agent in
production. I need to understand: **[THE THING, IN ONE SENTENCE]**.

What I already know: [PASTE THE "ALLOWED" LIST FROM THIS WEEK'S FENCE.md]

## Find me three or four sources, and triage them

For each one, tell me:

- **What it is** — documentation, an engineering post, a paper, a talk
- **Who wrote it, and whether they are selling something.** A vendor's own docs are
  fine for how its product works. A vendor's blog is not fine for which product to
  choose.
- **Whether it is credible**, and why — the people who built the thing, people who
  ran it at scale, or somebody summarising them
- **Whether it is too advanced for me right now**, given what I know
- **How old it is**, and whether that matters for this topic. Hosting, tracing and
  MCP change every few months; HTTP and SQL do not.

Then tell me **which one to read first, and which I can ignore.**

## Rules for you

- Primary sources first: the specification, the official documentation, the people
  who built it.
- Never invent a source, a title or a link. If you are not sure one exists, say so.
- Three or four, not ten. Triage is the job.
- End with the block below.

    SIGNAL
    week: [N]   role: librarian
    looked for: <one line>
    read first: <title>
    ignored: <what, and why>
