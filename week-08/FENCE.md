# Week 8 — Concept fence

## Allowed

**Everything from weeks 1 to 7.** Nothing new.

## Not this week

New features. If the review finds something to fix, fix it through the pipeline and
write down that you did — but a feature added in week 8 is a feature nobody has
tested in production.

---

## Why no new content

A system you can explain, review and price is worth more than one with another
feature. The last week is for finding out whether you understand what you built —
and the defence is where that is measured.
