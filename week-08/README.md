# Week 8 — The production review

> **Destination**
> You can show Desk live to someone who will try to break it, explain every part
> of it, say what it costs and what still gets through — and be believed.

**Project 2.** Nothing new is built this week. You write down what you built and
why, review it as if someone else had built it, and defend it on Friday.

---

## The week

| Day | Folder | What you'll be able to do |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | `day-1/` | Explain Desk to someone who has to change it without you |
| Tue | `day-2/` | Review it as a sceptic: what it defends, what breaks, what still gets through |
| Wed | `day-3/` | Say what it costs, how fast it is, and what gives out first |
| Thu | `day-4/` | Rehearse a live demo that includes a failure you cause |
| Fri | `milestone/` | **Project 2** — the demo and the defence |

---

## The rule this week

**Every claim has a number or a test behind it.** "It is secure" is not a claim.
"The attack suite's rate is 0.0 on fifteen runs against the primary model, and has
never been run against the fallback" is.
